The Cause

Lack of Affordable Housing

  • Rising rent makes it difficult for low-income individuals and families to maintain stable housing.
  • Unexpected expenses, such as medical bills or car repairs, can lead to eviction when people are already struggling financially.

Unemployment and Poverty

  • Lack of steady income makes it difficult to afford housing and other essential necessities.
  • Poverty can limit access to employment, education, and other opportunities needed to achieve financial stability.

Mental Health Challenges

  • Conditions such as anxiety, depression, and other severe mental illnesses can make it difficult to maintain employment and other daily responsibilities.

Substance Abuse Issues

  • Substance abuse can contribute to homelessness by damaging relationships, causing job loss, and creating health problems.
